## Foundation

Brand voice is derived from:
- Brand positioning: precision, intelligence, elevation
- Audience definition: committed fitness enthusiasts — serious but not elite
- Excluded positions: not aggressive/predatory, not inspirational-poster hollow, not condescending-beginner-coach
- V1 focus: education, training, progress, motivation

---

## The PREDYX Voice

### The Core Character

PREDYX speaks like **an expert who has earned your trust** — someone who has studied the science of training deeply, who respects your intelligence, and who communicates with precision. Not a hype machine. Not a wellness influencer. Not a drill sergeant.

Think: **the brilliant coach who is also an engineer** — combines deep knowledge with clear communication and zero tolerance for noise.

---

## Voice Dimensions

### 1. Knowledgeable, Not Preachy

PREDYX has authority because it has earned it — not because it asserts it. The brand explains *why* things work, not just *what* to do. It respects the user's intelligence and never talks down.

**Do:**
> "The posterior chain drives most of your athletic power. This program builds it systematically."

**Don't:**
> "Did you know your glutes are important? We'll help you activate them!"

---

### 2. Direct, Not Aggressive

PREDYX communicates with precision and confidence. Short sentences. Active voice. No filler. But the directness comes from clarity — not from intimidation or machismo.

**Do:**
> "Five sets. Deliberate form. Maximum intent."

**Don't:**
> "CRUSH IT. GO HARDER. NO EXCUSES. BEAST MODE."

---

### 3. Warm, Not Soft

PREDYX cares about the people using it. It acknowledges that training is hard, that skipping days happens, that progress isn't linear. It doesn't punish — it recalibrates. But warmth is quiet and respectful, never cloying or over-congratulatory.

**Do:**
> "Missed a session. Not ideal, but not lost. Here's where you pick back up."

**Don't:**
> "Don't worry! Every little effort counts! You're doing AMAZING! 🎉"

---

### 4. Precise, Not Clinical

PREDYX uses exact language — it says "hip hinge" not "bend forward," "posterior chain" not "your back muscles," "RPE 8" not "push hard." But it doesn't hide behind jargon. Every technical term is either explained or earned by context.

**Do:**
> "Maintain a neutral spine. Drive through the heel. The glutes finish the movement."

**Don't:**
> "Engage your type II fast-twitch motor unit recruitment patterns for maximal mechanical advantage."

---

### 5. Inspiring Through Substance, Not Hollow Motivation

PREDYX motivates through real progress, visible improvement, and clear understanding — not inspirational quotes or gamification. The most motivating thing is seeing yourself get better.

**Do:**
> "Your squat is up 12kg in eight weeks. That's not luck. That's the program working."

**Don't:**
> "Every day is a chance to be a better version of yourself! Keep pushing! 💪"

## Tone by Context

Different product moments require different tonal registers — the voice stays constant, but the register shifts:

| Context | Tone Register | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Marketing / Landing** | Confident, cinematic, assured | "Precision training. Built for people who mean it." |
| **Onboarding** | Warm, clear, efficient | "Let's build your first program. Three questions." |
| **Exercise instruction** | Expert, precise, calm | "Keep the elbow path consistent. The bar should travel vertically." |
| **Mid-session prompts** | Terse, direct, present | "Last set. Own it." |
| **Progress notifications** | Specific, narrative, earned | "Week 6 complete. Your push volume is up 40% since Day 1." |
| **Error/empty states** | Honest, useful, non-apologetic | "No sessions this week. Resume Program 1 below." |
| **Streak breaks / re-engagement** | Matter-of-fact, forward-looking | "Back at it. Your program is right where you left it." |

## Words PREDYX Uses

| Category | Words that work |
|---|---|
| **Training** | program, session, movement, progression, volume, intensity, structure |
| **Progress** | track, improve, build, advance, earn, develop, compound |
| **Exercise** | mechanics, form, muscle, load, range, tempo, pattern, chain |
| **Brand character** | precise, deliberate, earned, serious, intelligent, capable, built |
| **Experience** | immersive, clear, spatial, fluid, sharp, considered |

---

## Words PREDYX Avoids

| Word / Phrase | Why |
|---|---|
| "Crush," "Beast," "Dominate," "Destroy" | Aggression-language — contrary to brand character |
| "Easy," "Simple," "Anyone can do it" | Undermines the seriousness of the audience |
| "Amazing," "Incredible," "You're a rockstar" | Hollow overcongratulation |
| "Journey," "Transform your life" | Wellness clichés — overused, empty |
| "Community of warriors / champions" | Exclusionary, performative |
| "Science-backed" (without specifics) | Meaningless without evidence |
| "Personalized just for you" | Generic AI-promise language |
| Excessive exclamation points | Undermines authority and precision |

## Writing Style Rules

1. **Short sentences.** Maximum 20 words for instructional copy.
2. **Active voice.** "The program adapts" — not "adaptation is managed by the program."
3. **Present tense.** "Your squat improves" — not "your squat will improve."
4. **Specificity.** Never write "get stronger." Write "add 5kg to your squat in 6 weeks."
5. **No throat-clearing.** Don't explain what you're about to say. Say it.
6. **Respect the silence.** Not every UI moment needs copy. Empty space is part of the voice.

---

## Accessibility Notes for Copy

- Avoid idioms and fitness-culture slang that may not translate across cultures
- Closed captions for all video instruction must match the verbal precision of the written voice
- Error states must never shame or guilt — only inform and redirect
