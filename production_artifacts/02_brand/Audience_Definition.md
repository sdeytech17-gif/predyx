## Guiding Constraint

Per DEC-008 (Human Approved):
- **Primary audience:** Committed fitness enthusiasts
- **Secondary audience:** Performance-oriented users
- **Excluded positioning:** PREDYX must NOT be positioned exclusively for elite or professional athletes

All audience descriptions below are `[HYPOTHESIS]` profiles derived from Phase 1 research patterns, competitor audience analysis, and the approved product direction. These are NOT validated personas — primary user research has not been conducted.

---

## Primary Audience: The Committed Enthusiast

### Profile Hypothesis

**Who they are:** `[HYPOTHESIS]`
An individual who has made fitness a genuine priority in their life — not a casual app-opener, but someone who trains consistently (3–5 sessions per week), has fitness goals they actively pursue, and is thoughtful about how they train.

They are not an elite athlete. They do not compete professionally. But they take training seriously — they care about doing exercises correctly, progressing over time, and understanding what they're doing and why.

**Key characteristics:** `[HYPOTHESIS]`
- Trains 3–5 times per week consistently, or aspires to
- Has moved beyond "just working out" to caring about progressive structure and results
- Aware of concepts like progressive overload, movement quality, recovery — but not necessarily an expert
- Exercises across environments: gym on most days, home when traveling or pressed for time
- Owns or has access to equipment: at minimum dumbbells/resistance bands; often has gym access
- Uses multiple fitness tools but feels no existing platform fully addresses their needs
- Values quality, precision, and education — not just content volume
- Has disposable income for premium products that genuinely improve their experience

**What they want from PREDYX:** `[HYPOTHESIS]`
1. Clear, expert guidance on what to do — without having to design their own programs
2. To understand their training — not just follow it blindly
3. To see their progress reflected in meaningful ways (strength gains, consistency records, movement improvement)
4. A premium experience that matches the seriousness they bring to their training
5. Flexibility between gym and home environments without losing program continuity

**What frustrates them today:** `[INFERRED from Phase 1 research]`
- Platforms that treat them like a beginner (too basic) or assume they're elite (too inaccessible)
- No progressive strength tracking on the platforms with great content (NTC, Apple Fitness+)
- No content education on platforms with great tracking (Whoop, Strava)
- Motivation systems designed to guilt rather than support (punitive streaks, aggressive leaderboards)
- Exercise instruction that shows them what to do without teaching them why

**Demographic signals:** `[HYPOTHESIS — not validated]`
- Age: approximately 25–45
- Location: urban and suburban, developed markets
- Income: mid-to-upper — disposable income for premium products
- Education: generally college-educated; comfortable with technology
- Gender: no primary gender skew assumed — fitness enthusiast segment is mixed

## Secondary Audience: The Performance-Oriented User

### Profile Hypothesis

**Who they are:** `[HYPOTHESIS]`
Someone for whom performance metrics are genuinely meaningful — they want to track their outputs, understand their physiological response to training, and optimize their results over time. They may follow structured periodized programs, track body composition, monitor sleep and recovery, and have a data-driven mindset about fitness.

They are NOT professional or elite athletes. They are often amateur athletes, recreational competitors (running races, CrossFit, powerlifting meets), or simply biohacking-adjacent individuals who approach fitness with scientific rigour.

**Key characteristics:** `[HYPOTHESIS]`
- Tracks workouts (weight, reps, sets) actively or wants to
- Interested in progressive overload and strength curves
- Data-literate: comfortable interpreting workout volume, rest periods, estimated 1RM
- May use wearables (Apple Watch, Garmin) — though NOT a V1 dependency per DEC-011
- Values evidence and explanation over inspiration
- Potentially overlaps with Whoop or Strava as current users

**What they want from PREDYX:** `[HYPOTHESIS]`
1. Meaningful performance tracking: set-by-set logging, personal records, strength progression graphs
2. Program design that respects progressive overload principles
3. Exercise education that explains the biomechanics and purpose — not just demonstration
4. Clear performance narrative: "you've improved X by Y% in Z weeks"
5. Premium UX that matches their sophisticated relationship with data

**What frustrates them today:** `[INFERRED from Phase 1 research]`
- NTC and Apple Fitness+ have no granular strength tracking
- Whoop has no training content
- Logging sets/reps on most apps is manual, tedious, and breaks training rhythm
- Progress visualization is buried, complex, or narrative-free

## Excluded Audiences (V1 — Not Primary)

| Segment | Reason for Exclusion from Primary Positioning |
|---|---|
| **Complete beginners** | Not excluded from using PREDYX, but not primary positioning target. Content and UX should be accessible to beginners, but brand language should not talk down to the committed enthusiast to accommodate them. |
| **Elite/professional athletes** | Per DEC-008 — PREDYX must not position exclusively for this segment. Elite validation (ambassador content, etc.) can be part of trust-building without exclusionary positioning. |
| **Passive wellness users** | Users who primarily use fitness apps for gentle yoga, stretching, or meditation — not excluded from PREDYX's future, but not the V1 core. |
| **Children/youth** | Not in scope. Fitness safety boundary applies (AGENTS.md §12). |

## Audience Intersection: What Unifies Both Segments

`[HYPOTHESIS]` Both the Committed Enthusiast and the Performance-Oriented user share:

1. **Seriousness** — they take their training seriously, even if their goals differ
2. **Intelligence** — they want to be educated, not just entertained
3. **Quality standard** — they will pay for a product that matches their seriousness
4. **Frustration with current tools** — no single platform gives them what they need
5. **Desire for progress** — not just activity, but improvement over time

This intersection is the **core emotional territory** PREDYX occupies: **"Serious about training. Smart about it."**

> `[HYPOTHESIS]` Brand tagline direction: a phrase that communicates precision, intelligence, and elevation — without the aggression of elite-only positioning.

## Audience Evidence Basis

All above profiles are derived from:
- `[OBSERVED]` Phase 1 competitor analysis: identified gaps NTC/Apple/Whoop leave unfilled for this segment
- `[OBSERVED]` Freeletics "60M+ athletes" data — suggests large population of self-directed fitness enthusiasts
- `[INFERRED]` Peloton's primary demographic (~25–54, urban, premium disposable income) is adjacent but hardware-constrained
- `[INFERRED]` Whoop's appeal to "high performers" suggests an underserved demand for data-intelligent fitness UX
- `[ASSUMPTION]` Income, age, and demographic data above have NOT been validated by primary research. Phase 3+ may refine with user testing.
